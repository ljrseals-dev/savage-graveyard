Overview

This repository contains a sample chapter for the forthcoming IB textbook by Lewis Seals and Laila Star. The chapter introduces students to the discipline of iterative prompt improvement: forming a hypothesis, changing one variable at a time, measuring results with an eval suite, and recording every change in a changelog.

The sample is designed to demonstrate the textbook’s pedagogical style, including:

· Opening scenarios
· Guiding questions
· Margin glossaries
· Worked examples
· TOK connections
· Critical thinking exercises
· CAS connections
· Assessment preparation with marking schemes
· Chapter summaries and takeaways

---

Chapter Focus

Chapter 8: One Fix at a Time teaches students why changing one thing at a time produces more reliable improvements than changing many things at once.

Key concepts covered:

· Iteration
· Hypothesis formation
· Smallest change principle
· Regression detection
· Changelog discipline
· Causality in prompt engineering
· Prioritizing failing eval cases

Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, students should be able to:

· Explain why controlled, single-variable iteration improves reliability
· Form a testable hypothesis before changing a prompt
· Apply the smallest-change principle
· Identify regressions using a full eval suite
· Record changes in a structured changelog
· Prioritize failing eval cases using a defined framework
· Reflect on causality and the controlled experiment through a TOK lens
